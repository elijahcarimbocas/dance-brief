TL;DR

Different brief from the first one. No history, no level chart, no self-assessment. Seven clips from Cavallo classes between March and July, and in each one I've marked the second where the lead stops working.

Six of the breakdowns I can name: the duck under the follow's left arm, the pretzel, the crossbody into the Titanic, the lasso into the Titanic, the shoulder-check transfer behind my head, and the crossbody hold that reads as a travel. Two more I get rolled on and can't name at all. Those are at the bottom, waiting for you to tell me what they are.

01 Three things underneath all of it

Reading my own clips back, the six named problems collapse into three habits. Worth saying up front so we're not fixing six things one at a time.

Habit 01
I don't let go
My right hand stays on when the move needs it off. The duck becomes a lady presentation. The follow gets a shape she can't read, so she stalls, and I cover by inventing something.
Habit 02
Cuban bleeds in
When a linear On1 figure goes ambiguous, my hands find rueda. The pretzel turns into Setenta, sometimes Setenta Tres. It works, so I never learn the actual figure.
Habit 03
Entries, not exits
I can hold the Titanic once I'm in it. Getting in is where I die, from a crossbody and from a lasso both. Same for the shoulder-check transfer. It's always the doorway.

02 Where I break

Six named, then two I can't name. Every one links straight to the second in the clip.

2.1 Duck under the follow's left arm Jul 14 · whole clip Jul 16 · 0:09

I blow this one every time. Two failures stacked on each other. First, the follows often don't know what to do with the duck, so nothing comes back at me. Second, and this is mine, I don't release with my right hand. Hold the hand and the shape has nowhere to go, so it turns into a lady presentation instead. You can see the presentation version clearly at 0:09 on Jul 16.

2.2 The pretzel Jul 16 · 0:15

The pretzel shows up at 0:15 and I don't finish it. What I do instead is drop into Setenta, or into Setenta Tres where I just keep contact with both of the follow's hands. Both are Cuban rueda moves and both bail me out, which is exactly the problem. The exit works, so the pretzel never gets learned.
