02

The three lanes

Urban Bachata Fusion the way you two dance it.

Split these across the two Saturdays however you think best. I'd rather learn rules than memorize combos.

LANE 2.1salsachata

Port my salsa into bachata

  • A year of salsa vocabulary sitting next to my bachata: cross-body variants, copas, hammerlocks, hand tosses
  • Which figures port cleanly into bachata timing, and which don't
  • What changes in the footwork and the count when a figure crosses over
The ask: teach me the porting rule, then port two or three of my own figures with me.
LANE 2.2footwork & shines

Solo vocabulary that answers hers

  • My partnering is ahead of my solo work
  • When the song opens up and she styles, I mark basic until we reconnect
  • I want syncopations, breaks, and weight-change play of my own
The ask: a shine set I can drill in the week between the two lessons.
LANE 2.3sensual control

Supported, not muscled

  • Body waves, back leans, low drops: I know the shapes from class, I want the lead's mechanics
  • Where the impulse starts, how much is torso versus arms
  • How to take a follow into a lean or drop so it feels supported instead of muscled - Brief I's clarity-versus-aggression question, applied to sensual
The ask: walk me through the back leans and drops, then put them on me until they're clean. Section 03 has the exact spots.
